# ✈️ Lab 4 — AI Task Classification

## 📌 Overview

This lab focuses on **AI Task Classification** in the aviation-business domain. The objective is to understand different AI capabilities and identify which capability is most appropriate for a particular business requirement.

The four main AI capabilities explored are:

* 🔮 **Prediction**
* 🗂️ **Classification**
* 💡 **Recommendation**
* ✍️ **Generation**

The lab demonstrates that an aviation manager should first understand the business problem and then select the appropriate AI capability.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

* Understand the difference between Prediction, Classification, Recommendation, and Generation.
* Classify aviation-business tasks according to their main AI capability.
* Apply AI capabilities to practical airline scenarios.
* Develop an understanding of business-focused AI selection.
* Learn why the business requirement should be identified before selecting an AI solution.

```text
Prediction       → What is likely to happen?

```

This memory framework was used to make the four AI capabilities easier to distinguish.

## ✈️ Aviation Task Examples

### 🔮 Prediction

Prediction is used when the system estimates a future outcome.

**Examples:**

* Estimate whether a morning flight will arrive late.
* Forecast passenger numbers for a long weekend.
* Estimate passenger demand for a route next month.
* Forecast demand during the winter holiday period.

### 🗂️ Classification

Classification places information into predefined categories.

**Examples:**

* Sort passenger messages into baggage, payment, booking, and service categories.
* Identify feedback as positive, negative, or neutral.
* Categorize complaints into baggage, staff, delay, food, and booking issues.

### 💡 Recommendation

Recommendation suggests an option based on available information or passenger requirements.

**Examples:**

* Suggest a suitable seat.
* Recommend an add-on service.
* Suggest a suitable paid service to a passenger.
* Recommend a route-related promotional offer.

### ✍️ Generation

Generation is used when the AI needs to create new content.

**Examples:**

* Create a welcome message for passengers.
* Draft a staff email.
* Write a social-media post for a new destination.
* Create a passenger email.

The lab's examples demonstrate how the purpose of the task determines the appropriate AI capability.

## 📚 Key Concepts

### Prediction

Uses available information to estimate a future event or value.

**Example:** Forecasting passenger demand for an airline route.

### Classification

Places information into predefined categories.

**Example:** Sorting passenger complaints into baggage, booking, or delay categories.

### Recommendation

Suggests an option based on relevant information or requirements.

**Example:** Suggesting an additional travel service.

### Generation

Creates new content such as text, images, audio, video, or presentations.

## 🔄 AI Task Selection Process

The main learning from this lab can be represented as:

```text
Business Requirement
        ↓
Understand the Task
        ↓
Identify the Main Objective
        ↓
Select AI Capability
        ↓
Apply AI Solution
        ↓
Review the Result
```

Selecting an AI tool or capability before understanding the requirement can lead to an unsuitable solution.
